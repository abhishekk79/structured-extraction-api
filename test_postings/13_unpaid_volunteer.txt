Volunteer Grant Writer

Riverside Community Food Bank is seeking a Volunteer Grant Writer to help
us apply for foundation and government grants. This is an unpaid volunteer
position, roughly 5-8 hours per week, fully remote.

You'll research grant opportunities, draft proposals, and work with our
part-time program director to gather supporting data.

What we're looking for:
- Experience writing grant proposals or similar persuasive writing
- Comfortable with independent research
- Passion for food security work

This role is unpaid, but we're happy to provide a letter of reference and
can accommodate school volunteer-hour requirements.
