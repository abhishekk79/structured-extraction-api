Solutions Architect

Vantage Systems is hiring a Solutions Architect to work with our enterprise
customers on integration projects.

Work arrangement: this role is primarily remote, but you should expect to
travel to customer sites and our Chicago HQ roughly once a month, sometimes
more during major rollouts.

Requirements:
- 5+ years in a pre-sales or solutions architecture role
- Experience with REST APIs, webhooks, and SSO integrations (SAML/OIDC)
- Strong client-facing communication skills
- Willingness to travel up to 25%

Compensation: $130,000 - $150,000 base, plus a variable bonus tied to
customer implementation success.
