Product Designer

Meridian Health is hiring a Product Designer to join our clinical tools team.
Fully remote, US timezones preferred.

You'll own the design of internal dashboards used by nurses and care
coordinators. We're a small design team (2 people) so you'll do everything
from user research to high-fidelity mockups to light front-end implementation
in Figma-to-code workflows.

Must-haves:
- 4+ years of product design experience, ideally in healthcare or B2B SaaS
- Portfolio showing end-to-end design process
- Experience working directly with engineers, not just handing off specs

We don't publish salary ranges publicly but are happy to discuss compensation
early in the process. Full benefits, unlimited PTO, equity.
