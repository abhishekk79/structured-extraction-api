Customer Success Manager

Harborline Software is hiring a Customer Success Manager. This role can be
based out of any of our three offices: New York, NY; Austin, TX; or
Denver, CO. Fully remote candidates within the US will also be considered.

You'll own a portfolio of mid-market accounts, run quarterly business
reviews, and partner with sales on renewals and expansion.

Requirements:
- 3+ years in a customer success or account management role
- Experience with a CRM (we use HubSpot)
- Comfortable presenting to VP- and C-level stakeholders

Salary: $85,000 - $100,000 per year, plus a variable bonus.
