Vice President of Engineering

Halcyon Biotech is looking for a Vice President of Engineering to lead our
technology organization through our next phase of growth. Onsite in
San Diego, CA, with flexibility for occasional remote work.

Reporting directly to the CTO, you will set technical direction across our
three engineering teams, build out our engineering leadership bench, and
represent engineering at the executive staff level. This is a
organization-shaping role for someone who has scaled a team through
hypergrowth before and wants to do it again.

We're looking for someone who has:
- Led engineering organizations of 50+ people
- Operated at the VP or SVP level previously, ideally in biotech, health
  tech, or another regulated industry
- A track record of building durable engineering culture, not just
  shipping features
- Comfort operating as a peer to the CEO, CTO, and CFO

Base salary: $260,000 - $310,000, plus a significant equity grant and an
annual executive bonus target of 30%.
