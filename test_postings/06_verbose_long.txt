Senior Platform Engineer — Infrastructure & Reliability

About Us
Cascadia Freight is a logistics technology company operating across North
America. We move freight data, not just freight. Our platform team keeps the
lights on for every other engineering team at the company, and we take that
responsibility seriously. We were founded in 2016, raised our Series C in
2024, and now process over 40 million shipment events per day across our
core systems.

The Role
We are looking for a Senior Platform Engineer to join our Infrastructure &
Reliability group. You will be embedded in a team of 6 engineers responsible
for the reliability, scalability, and security of our core platform, which
underpins every product surface at Cascadia. This is a highly cross-
functional role: you will partner closely with product engineering teams,
security, and data platform to ensure our systems scale gracefully and fail
safely.

Responsibilities
- Design, build, and operate infrastructure supporting 40M+ daily events
- Own our Kubernetes platform, including cluster upgrades, autoscaling
  policy, and multi-tenancy guardrails
- Drive incident response and blameless postmortems
- Improve observability across metrics, logs, and traces
- Partner with security on hardening our container supply chain
- Mentor mid-level and junior engineers on infrastructure best practices
- Participate in an on-call rotation (1 week in 6)

Required Qualifications
- 6+ years of experience in infrastructure, SRE, or platform engineering
- Deep hands-on experience with Kubernetes in production at scale
- Strong knowledge of AWS (EKS, VPC, IAM, RDS, S3)
- Proficiency with Terraform or another IaC tool
- Experience with Prometheus, Grafana, and distributed tracing (OpenTelemetry
  or similar)
- Solid Go or Python skills for tooling and automation
- Experience with CI/CD systems (we use GitHub Actions and ArgoCD)
- Familiarity with Kafka or another event streaming platform
- Comfortable with Docker, Helm, and service mesh concepts (we use Istio)
- Excellent written communication for RFCs and postmortems

Nice to Have
- Experience with cost optimization at scale (FinOps)
- Prior experience in logistics, e-commerce, or another high-throughput
  domain
- Contributions to open source infrastructure tooling
- Security certifications (CKS, AWS Security Specialty)

Location & Work Arrangement
This role is based out of our Seattle, WA headquarters. We operate on a
hybrid schedule: Tuesday through Thursday in office, with Monday and Friday
flexible to work from home. Relocation assistance is available for the right
candidate.

Compensation & Benefits
The salary range for this role is $165,000 to $205,000 per year, based on
experience and interview performance. In addition to base salary, this role
includes:
- Meaningful equity grant
- 100% covered health, dental, and vision insurance for employees
- 401(k) with 4% match
- $2,000 annual learning and development stipend
- Unlimited PTO (with a 3-week minimum encouraged)
- Fully paid parental leave
