Marketing Coordinator

Bloom & Co, a boutique event planning agency, is hiring a Marketing
Coordinator to join our small team in Denver, CO. This is an in-office role.

You'll manage our social media calendar, coordinate with photographers and
vendors, and help plan our seasonal open-house events.

What we're looking for:
- 1-2 years of marketing or coordination experience
- Comfortable with Canva, Instagram, and basic email marketing tools
- Highly organized and deadline-driven

This is an entry-level role. Salary: $48,000 - $54,000 per year.
